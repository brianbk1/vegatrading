Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This policy explains what Vega Trading ("we," "us") collects when you use vegatrading.ai, and what you can do about it.

Information You Provide

The ticker, strike, option price, and other inputs you enter into the analyzer are used to calculate results during your session. No account is required, and we do not ask for your name, email, brokerage credentials, or any information that would identify your actual trading account or positions.

Automatically Collected Information

Standard technical data — IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, pages visited — is collected via analytics and hosting logs, as with nearly all websites.

AI-Assisted Analysis

Some calculations are processed through Anthropic's API. Your input values are sent to generate the analysis and are not used to build a profile of you or retained for purposes beyond producing your result. See Anthropic's privacy policy for how they handle API data.

Advertising and Google AdSense

This site is supported by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and others.
  • Opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of additional third-party vendors at aboutads.info.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage. This data does not directly identify you. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Data We Do Not Collect

No brokerage credentials, no account numbers, no actual position data. Vega Trading has no way to see your real portfolio and does not ask for one.

Your Choices

  • Block or clear cookies in your browser settings.
  • Opt out of personalized ads via the links above.

Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top reflects the latest revision.
