Before You Write

Questions about your specific position or account — Vega Trading doesn't have access to your brokerage account and can't see your actual position. Direct those questions to your broker.

A Greeks calculation looks off — tell us the ticker, strike, expiration, and the price you entered. Estimates are approximations; if a number is meaningfully wrong rather than just approximate, we want to know.

Feature requests — genuinely useful for prioritizing what gets built next. Tell us what trade you were trying to evaluate and what the tool couldn't tell you.
